# Story

## Premise

Humans burned through their own planet with technology. Now their fleet travels to other worlds and **terraforms** them into copies of the home they destroyed — concrete, smog, grey.

Clown Planet is their next target: a happy world with no technology, where clowns have magic powered by joy and laughter.

## What the humans do

1. **Terraform Engines** pour grey (concrete, smog, fog) outward from the landing site. Anything the grey covers loses its colour and magic.
2. Robots break too often, so humans **capture clowns and lock them inside mechanical toy shells**. The trapped clown's joy powers the toy; a **control chip** makes it obey.

That is why the toys are creepy: they move like clowns, and sometimes giggle at the wrong moment.

## The motive

Three sisters from a travelling circus troupe. On opening night the grey reaches their town and the whole troupe is captured. The sisters escape because they were up in the trapeze tent.

Two goals that pull against each other:

- **Free the troupe** — find friends and family inside the toys. Bosses are giant toys with someone they love inside, so they can't just smash them: break the control chip and pull the clown out.
- **Stop the grey** — destroy each region's Terraform Engine before the grey swallows the last village. The grey keeps spreading while they search.

## Acts

### Act 1 — The Grey Arrives
- Opening night show in **Candy Meadows** (tutorial: movement, car, first toy fight).
- Grey wave hits, troupe is captured, sisters escape in the clown car (first chase).
- Base camp: the torn circus tent on the edge of the grey.

### Act 2 — Pushing Back
- Open world: **Autumn Woods**, **Sky Islands**, **Drained Desert**, **Haunted Fairground** (any order after Autumn).
- Each region: find the engine, free villagers, beat the boss (a troupe member), destroy the engine.
- Freed troupe members return to the tent and give abilities, shops, upgrades.
- Midpoint reveal: the convoys all go to one place — the **Toy Factory**.

### Act 3 — The Factory and the Landing Site
- **Toy Factory**: where clowns are turned into toys. Puzzle-heavy, all three sisters together.
- Discover the **kingclown** (their mentor) is being built into the Commander's mech.
- **Landing Site**: Master Engine and the final boss.

## Ending

The kingclown is freed, the Master Engine shuts down, the grey cracks and colour floods back.
Some humans leave. A few stay and help build a new world where technology learns to live with magic (solarpunk ending).

## Tone

- World and heroes: cute, colourful, funny.
- Enemies: genuinely dangerous and creepy (tactical, smart AI).
- Never preachy: humans are desperate, not cartoon-evil.
